How it works

Four steps from first message to a lit world.

  1. Tell us about your world

    Send your server or world details and what you want lit or built.

  2. We look and plan

    We review your builds and agree a lighting style with you.

  3. We install

    Our team places the lighting and builds the redstone, power and control rooms. We do the full install properly, not just place a light and a switch.

  4. You enjoy the night

    Players explore your world at night with every area lit.
